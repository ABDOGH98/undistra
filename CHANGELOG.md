# Changelog

All notable changes to Undistra are listed here.
Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-07

First release. 🎉

### Added
- **App blocking:** blocked apps close as soon as they open during a session. A passcode opens them for 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.
- **Website blocking** in every browser through `/etc/hosts`, including `www.` and mobile versions, with one-click categories: Social media, Video & streaming, News, Shopping.
- **Focus sessions** of 25, 50, 90 minutes or any length, with a progress ring, countdown and end time.
- **Schedules** with ready-made options (weekday mornings, afternoons, full workday, screen-free nights), including overnight schedules.
- **Intentions:** write what you're working on, and see it again when you're tempted.
- **Stats:** time focused today, completed sessions and day streak.
- **Do Not Disturb** during focus, through the Shortcuts app.
- **Passcode protection:** salted hash stored in the Keychain, with a 60-second lockout after 3 wrong tries.
- **Anti-cheat:** during a session you can't remove blocked items, turn off schedules, or quit the app.
- **Welcome guide** for first-time setup.
- **Menu bar** with quick start, time left and today's stats.
- **Open at login** option.
- Universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
